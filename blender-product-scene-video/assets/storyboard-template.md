## Grid acceptance checklist

- [ ] All shots appear in one grid image
- [ ] The master grid covers every confirmed scene from opening through final frame
- [ ] Every adjacent scene has an explicit entry/exit continuity handoff
- [ ] Panels follow chronological reading order
- [ ] Every panel has a visible scene/shot id
- [ ] Every panel carries its exact spoken line, or an explicit no-voiceover label
- [ ] Every panel maps to an exact script beat and explains its design reason
- [ ] Every real UI panel cites a verified screenshot or recording timestamp
- [ ] Every screenshot citation preserves the exact filename, extension, case, punctuation, and parentheses
- [ ] Screenshot filenames are used as the canonical UI-state labels
- [ ] Generated crops, annotations, and boards are stored outside `UI参考图/`
- [ ] No required product beat remains `missing`
- [ ] Every moving camera/product/UI/light/transition has a labeled trajectory arrow
- [ ] Every panel boundary says `ONE TAKE` or names the edit method
- [ ] Every phone rotation shows the local axis triad, named axis, signed angle, and start/end pose
- [ ] Every panel lists camera, product, focus, lighting, timing, and continuity targets
- [ ] Unknown scale-dependent values are marked `TBD in Blender preflight`
- [ ] The grid includes one consistent arrow and light legend
- [ ] The legend explains every color, line style, focus/lock symbol, and `S/E` mark
- [ ] Every line or arrow identifies its movement/light owner and direction
- [ ] The visual treatment is a rough sketch rather than a polished render preview
- [ ] All panels use the intended delivery aspect ratio
- [ ] Phone pose, UI priority, lighting, and background are judgeable
- [ ] Labels do not cover important product content
